## Reading

A positive worst-case component means the rectangular geometrization is aligned with the ramp field over every element of `N(F_level)`, which is the numerical content of `thm:R1ABlattice`. A negative one identifies a wall the combinatorial map orients one way and the field crosses the other -- reported per wall in the JSON.
